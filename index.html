What these projects demonstrate

These are experiments in product judgement, not claims of production deployment. Each project examines an architectural or product decision, measures behaviour and documents limitations.

Three recurring questions: How do we know the output is good? What should the model be allowed to do? How should the experience respond when the model is uncertain or wrong?
